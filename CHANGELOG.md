# What changed in QuestCut

Version numbers are **BIG . SMALL . FIX**:

- **BIG** goes up for big changes (a new way of working; old projects might need converting).
- **SMALL** goes up when new features are added.
- **FIX** goes up for bug fixes only.

The version you have is shown in the window title and in **Help → About QuestCut**.
Each saved project also records which version saved it.

## 1.0.1 (18 November 2026)

The first numbered version. It includes everything built so far:

**Music (new in this version)**
- 13 original orchestral pieces made by QuestCut's own virtual orchestra (harp, lute, flute, strings, brass,
  choir, timpani and other drums, bells), in stereo with a hall echo. Loops repeat seamlessly.
- Medieval quest (RuneScape-like): Quest giver, Quest accepted, Setting off, Tavern jig, Sneaking,
  Battle: drums and horns, Quest complete: big fanfare.
- Dark fantasy (Elden Ring-like): Monster steals the treasure, Dread, Dark boss, Enemy felled, Resting place,
  Lament.
- Quest conversation (Q) and fight (K) now use the new music.
- Improved built-in sounds replace old saved copies automatically.

**Editing**
- Timeline with cut, trim, move, copy/paste, undo, keyframes.
- Two cameras: sync by sound, switch camera, picture-in-picture.
- Insert mode, insert empty time, remove a marked stretch of time from every track.
- Box selection and groups; every one-click sequence comes out as a group.
- Close gaps; combine several scene projects into one movie; pack a project for another computer.

**Game look**
- Game HUD (health/mana/XP/boss bars, name tags, dialogue, quests, character cards, score, timer, ammo,
  minimap, kill feed, prompts, menus, level-up, damage numbers, frames) in three styles.
- Spell / attack events that lower a health bar at the moment of the hit.
- 42 game-inspired elements: RuneScape (chat box with talking head, Select an Option, overhead bars with damage
  splats, Quest complete scroll, chat messages; quest conversation and fight in one click), Pokémon-style
  (battle start, HP boxes, battle text and menus, catch, Who's that …?, evolution, index entry, badge,
  level-up), Stardew Valley, Skyrim, Elden Ring, Zelda, Street Fighter, Minecraft.
- Automatic cut-out of a person for silhouettes; pictures from files or video frames.
- Looks, vignettes, touch-ups, freeze-frame character cards, title / Game Over / credits cards, transitions.

**Sound and export**
- 33 game sounds, voice-over recording, fades, music ducking under voices.
- Export to MP4 with GPU encoding when available (NVIDIA, Apple, Intel).

**Other**
- Guide tab with 14 hands-on lessons and a practice project.
- 134 automatic tests.
